# Model card: retail demand forecasting baseline

## Intended use

Short- to medium-horizon inventory planning for daily retail demand. The model supports aggregate demand or a selected store/item slice.

## Model

Additive Holt-Winters exponential smoothing with weekly seasonality and damped trend. The forecast interval is generated by bootstrapping in-sample residuals and is intended as a planning interval.

## Evaluation

The trainer uses expanding-window rolling-origin backtesting with MAE, RMSE, and WAPE. This avoids random splits and prevents future observations from entering training folds.

## Limitations

The baseline does not yet use holidays, price, promotions, weather, stock availability, or supplier lead times. A zero-filled missing date is interpreted as zero demand, which is inappropriate if missingness reflects a data outage. The interval is empirical rather than a formal statistical confidence interval.

## Production considerations

Monitor data freshness, missingness, forecast bias, WAPE by store/item, and stockout-censored demand. Refit on a scheduled cadence and compare against a seasonal-naive benchmark before production adoption.
